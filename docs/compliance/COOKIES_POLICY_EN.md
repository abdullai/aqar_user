# Cookies Policy

The platform uses cookies for security, analytics, and performance.

## Cookie Types

### Necessary Cookies

Required for platform operation.

### Analytics Cookies

Used for analytics and performance improvement.

### Marketing Cookies

Used for marketing and personalization.

## Consent Management

Users may:

- Accept cookies
- Reject optional cookies
- Modify preferences
- Withdraw consent

> Optional preferences sync via Settings → Consent management when the backend migration is applied.
