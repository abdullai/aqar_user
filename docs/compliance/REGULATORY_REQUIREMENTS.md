# Regulatory Requirements (Saudi Real-Estate Platform)

## Commercial & sector activity

- Valid **commercial registration** including brokerage / marketing activity as applicable (e.g. ISIC **682010** where relevant to your licensed scope).
- **Saudi Business Center (MC)** documentation as required for your entity type.

## Personal data (PDPL-oriented posture)

- Lawful bases for processing (contract, legal obligation, consent where needed).
- Data subject rights channel (support + privacy mailbox).
- DPIA / RoPA style documentation maintained by the operator (outside this repo).

## REGA (Real Estate General Authority)

- Advertiser / listing verification workflows aligned with your REGA integration roadmap.
- Complaint handling traceability (in-app `regc_user_complaints` + mailboxes).
- License / permit validation steps surfaced in listing flows (app-specific).

## Cybersecurity baseline (NCA ECC / best practice alignment)

- Access control, logging, incident response, secure SDLC — see `SECURITY.md` and `INCIDENT_RESPONSE.md`.

> Final obligations depend on entity classification, product scope, and regulator guidance — validate with qualified Saudi counsel.
